We understand the importance of personal information and health data to you and process your information lawfully, fairly, necessarily, and in good faith.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use the Awak Health iOS app (the “App”) and **Awak Health smart wearable devices** (including but not limited to smart rings and bands, the “Device”), and your rights.

**Please read carefully. All App features require registration and login; unauthenticated access is not provided. If you do not agree, do not register for or use the App and device-related features.**

## 1. Information We Collect

We follow a “minimum necessary” principle. Health-related data is **sensitive personal information** and is collected only when necessary for corresponding features and with your authorization.

### 1.1 Information You Provide

| Type | Content | Purpose |
|------|---------|---------|
| Account | Mobile number, verification code, nickname, avatar, gender, height, weight, date of birth; when using **one-click login with local mobile number**, the verified local mobile number obtained via carrier gateway and Aliyun Number Authentication | Registration, login, health metrics, profile |
| Emergency contact | Name, phone number (optional) | Safety-related scenarios you configure |
| Feedback & support | Text, images you submit | Issue handling and follow-up |
| Nutrition records | Meal text, photos for recognition (if used) | Nutrition analysis and display |

### 1.2 Information from Device Sensors

After you bind the Device and authorize collection, sensors and Bluetooth sync may collect:

- **Heart & circulation**: Heart rate, resting heart rate, HRV, SpO₂, ECG and waveform summaries, algorithm risk hints (reference only, not diagnosis)
- **Activity**: Steps, distance, calories, exercise type and intensity, session records
- **Metabolism & activity load**: MET time series and derived activity load statistics (depending on device support)
- **Sleep**: Sleep/wake times, duration, sleep stages, quality-related scores
- **Body state**: Skin temperature, stress index, blood pressure, glucose, uric acid, blood lipids (total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL, LDL, etc., depending on model and settings)
- **Body composition**: Weight, BMI, body fat %, muscle mass/rate, body water, visceral fat, basal metabolism, bone mass/protein, etc. (depending on model and settings)
- **Comprehensive health check (micro checkup)**: Multiple metrics and result summaries collected in a single measurement (not a hospital physical exam)
- **Device info**: Model, firmware, Bluetooth MAC, battery, sync logs, fault records

### 1.3 Technical Information Generated During Use

- OS version, app version, device identifiers, IP address, crash logs, request timestamps
- **Product analytics** (after you agree to this policy): Feature usage and page views, **excluding** raw heart rate, SpO₂, sleep stages, and other raw health data

## 2. How We Use Information

| Purpose | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Core health services | Sync, metrics, trends, scores, reports, and reminders via **Awak proprietary rules, algorithms, and health models** |
| Personalized suggestions (AI-assisted) | After core analysis, submit selected information to **entrusted AI inference services in China** for personalized health, exercise, nutrition, and vitality references (see §2.2) |
| AI Health Butler chat | Chat-based personalized health and lifestyle references; **not medical diagnosis, medication, or treatment advice** (see §2.3) |
| One-click login with local mobile number | In **Mainland China** (cn app bundle), after you check the relevant agreements, verify your local mobile number via carrier gateway and Aliyun Number Authentication for login/registration (see §2.4); falls back to SMS OTP on failure |
| Awak model & product improvement | With legal basis and your authorization, use de-identified data to improve Awak health models and stability |
| Security | Authentication, risk control, abnormal login detection |
| Legal obligations | Cooperation with regulators, courts, or law enforcement as required by law |

### 2.1 Algorithms and Intelligent Technology

**Core analysis** is developed by us, including sensor signal processing, health metrics, sleep/activity/recovery assessment, body composition assessment, micro checkup comprehensive reference, metabolic load (MET) analysis, vitality trends, and anomaly detection, running on-device or in China cloud. Personalized suggestions rely primarily on these Awak capabilities.

**AI assistance** generates more tailored references and explanatory text after core analysis. It **does not replace** Awak core health analysis and **does not constitute medical diagnosis**.

We **do not** use your health data for targeted advertising, insurance underwriting marketing, or sale to data brokers.

### 2.2 Data Scope for AI Inference

To provide personalized suggestions and explanations under your authorization, we may submit the following to entrusted AI inference services in China (**data does not leave China**):

**May be submitted:**

| Category | Examples (depending on enabled features) |
|----------|----------------------------------------|
| Health metrics & statistics | Heart rate, resting HR, HRV, SpO₂, sleep score/duration/stages, activity and exercise summaries, stress, vitality-related metrics, nutrition summaries, body composition summaries, micro checkup summaries, uric acid/lipid trend summaries, MET/activity load summaries, etc. |
| Awak analysis conclusions | Trends, scores, recovery and fatigue summaries from Awak algorithms |
| Basic body info | Gender, date of birth (or age range), height, weight |

**Not submitted to AI inference:**

- Mobile number, name, ID number, account ID, device MAC, or other **directly identifiable** fields;
- ECG **raw waveforms** or other unnecessarily granular data;
- Data for advertising, marketing, or purposes unrelated to core services.

Health metrics and statistics may be sensitive personal information; we submit them only within your authorization, for personalized health reference, on a minimum-necessary basis, with contractual restrictions on processors.

### 2.3 AI Health Butler Chat Service

“AI Health Butler” is an in-app chat feature through which you may interact with an intelligent dialogue service for personalized health and lifestyle references.

**Scope**: This feature provides **health and lifestyle references only** (e.g. daily knowledge and suggestions on rest, diet, and exercise). It **does not provide, and does not constitute, medical diagnosis, medication, or treatment advice**. If your questions involve disease diagnosis, medication, or treatment plans, we will not answer them and recommend that you consult a qualified healthcare provider.

**Information you should not actively provide**: To protect your information security, we **do not recommend** that you actively provide medical-related sensitive information such as medical history, prior diagnoses, or medications in chat. Even if you provide such information, this feature **will not** answer questions based on it.

**How we process data**: To respond to your questions, we may combine the health metrics, Awak analysis summaries, and basic body information listed in §2.2 with **text you actively enter in chat**, and submit them to entrusted AI inference services in China to generate replies (**data does not leave China**; exclusion rules are the same as §2.2 — no directly identifiable fields, no ECG raw waveforms, etc.). Chat content is encrypted and stored on our servers in China to maintain conversation continuity, content safety review, and feature improvement, for the same retention period as your account (see §6).

**Liability**:

- If your information disclosed in chat is leaked due to **our failure to fulfill necessary security obligations** (e.g. system vulnerabilities, storage or transmission incidents), we will bear liability as required by law;
- If you **voluntarily disclose** medical-related sensitive information such as medical history or medications in chat, we are **not liable** for the nature of such information or consequences of your voluntary disclosure (where our security obligations were not breached). Please carefully consider whether to share such information in chat.

### 2.4 One-Click Login with Local Mobile Number

“One-click login with local mobile number” is available in **Mainland China** (cn app bundle). After you **check** the User Agreement, Privacy Policy, and **age confirmation**, it verifies your local mobile number via the **Aliyun Number Authentication SDK** and **telecom carriers** (China Mobile / China Unicom / China Telecom, depending on your SIM) to complete login or silent registration.

**Process (summary)**:

1. After you check the relevant agreements and confirm your age, we initialize the Number Authentication SDK and pre-fetch credentials on the client;
2. When you tap one-click login, the SDK presents the **carrier authorization page** (carrier terms are provided by the carrier and **are not replaced** by this policy);
3. After you confirm authorization, the SDK returns a login token; our **servers in China** call Aliyun Number Authentication to verify the token, obtain your **local mobile number**, and complete login or registration;
4. If pre-fetch fails, you cancel authorization, or the network environment is unsuitable (e.g. no cellular data), the feature is unavailable or fails; you may still log in via **SMS verification code** and **will not be blocked** from using the App.

**Information we directly obtain**: The **mobile number** verified through the above process (for login, registration, and security checks).

**Information the SDK and carriers may collect** (necessary for one-click login; collected on-device per Aliyun and carrier official disclosures; **we do not repeat every technical field here**):

| Category | Examples | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Network and device | Network type, IP address, device manufacturer, device model, mobile OS | Terminal type, network channel, and one-click login adaptation |
| SIM / carrier | SIM state, carrier type | Identify available carrier gateway; some fields may be **collected but not stored** (per SDK official docs) |

**Compliance timing**: We initialize the Number Authentication SDK and pre-fetch credentials **only after** you **agree to this policy** and **check the User Agreement and age confirmation**; we **do not** call related SDK APIs before that.

**Storage and entrusted processing**: Mobile numbers are stored as account information **in the People’s Republic of China** (see §6); server-side token verification is entrusted to **Alibaba Cloud Computing Co., Ltd.** (Number Authentication) in China; **data does not leave China**.

**Carrier agreements**: Carrier authentication terms on the one-click login authorization page are provided and displayed by the **relevant telecom carrier**. Please read carrier terms as well. Appendix B lists related SDKs and official privacy policy links.

## 6. Storage and Security

### 6.1 Location and Retention

- Personal and health data stored on **Alibaba Cloud servers in the People’s Republic of China**;
- While your account is active, we retain data needed for trend comparison;
- After account deletion and expiry of the 7-day cooling-off period, we delete or anonymize linked personal data;
- **Anonymized audit logs** (without identifiable health details) retained up to **3 years** for security and disputes.

### 6.2 Security Measures

TLS/SSL in transit; access control and encryption (e.g. AES) at rest. No internet transmission is absolutely secure.

### 6.3 Cross-Border Transfer

**We do not currently provide personal information overseas.** If cross-border transfer is needed in the future, we will conduct security assessments and obtain separate consent as required by law.
