# Awakening Will (hangzhou) Health Technology Co., Ltd Wearable Device Safety Notice

**Version**: v1.0 (Awak Health smart wearable devices)  
**Updated**: 2026-06-18  
**Applicable products**: Awak Health smart wearable devices (including but not limited to smart rings and bands; specific models and features per packaging and included manual)

*English translation of the official Chinese version.*

---

Dear user, for your safety and proper use of **Awak Health smart wearable devices** (the “Device”), please read and follow this notice before use.

**This Device is a consumer electronics product, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat disease.**

Failure to follow this guidance may cause skin irritation, device damage, measurement errors, or—in extreme cases—safety incidents.

---

## 1. Restrictions for Specific Users

### 1. Skin Allergy or Injury

- Do not wear if you are allergic to metal, silicone, or similar materials, or if the wearing area has wounds, eczema, sunburn, or other skin damage.
- Stop wearing and consult a doctor if redness, itching, rash, or discomfort occurs.

### 2. Implanted Active Medical Devices

- The Device contains electronics and Bluetooth RF; charging accessories may contain magnetic components.
- If you have an implanted pacemaker, defibrillator, insulin pump, neurostimulator, or similar **active implant**, consult your doctor before use and keep the Device at least **15 cm (6 inches)** from the implant site.
- Follow electromagnetic compatibility and safety guidance from your medical device manufacturer.

### 3. Pregnancy and Special Physiological Periods

- Heart rate, body temperature, and other metrics may vary significantly during pregnancy. Device data **must not be used for medical monitoring or diagnosis during pregnancy**. Seek medical advice if unwell.

---

## 2. Wearing and Environment

### 1. Proper Wear

- Optical/bio sensors need good skin contact; too loose or too tight may affect heart rate, SpO₂, etc. **Wear position and method per your product manual.**
- Clean the Device and skin regularly; keep dry; avoid prolonged enclosure with sweat or detergent.

### 2. Rotation of Wearing Site

- Briefly remove the Device daily or every other day, clean and dry, then **change wearing position or side** (if applicable) to let skin breathe.

### 3. Water and Dust Resistance

- **Water/dust ratings and depth limits per your model’s packaging and manual** (some models may be rated IP68).
- Generally avoid diving, high-pressure water, surfing, hot saunas/springs (typically >40°C), etc.
- Leather, fabric, or other non-metal parts (if any) are usually not water-resistant.

### 4. Chemicals and Mechanical Stress

- Avoid sunscreen, repellent, perfume, alcohol hand sanitizer, strong cleaners, etc., which may corrode coatings or seals.
- Avoid impact, drops, twisting; intense exercise or crushing may deform the Device, damage sensors, or cause discomfort.

---

## 3. Charging and Battery Safety

1. **Charging**: Use the included cable and **5V standard** adapter (per manual). Do not use fast chargers exceeding rated voltage.
2. **Environment**: Charge in ventilated, dry, non-flammable areas; **do not** charge under bedding, pillows, or in sealed bags.
3. **Abnormalities**: If the Device or charger is abnormally hot, smokes, swells, or smells, disconnect power immediately, keep away from flammables, and contact official support. **Do not** disassemble, crush, or puncture the battery.
4. **Contacts**: Keep charging contacts and skin-facing surfaces clean and dry; sweat or liquid may cause poor contact or corrosion.

---

## 4. Data and Measurement Notice

### 1. Sensor Interference

- **Fit**, dark tattoos, moles, scars, dense hair, large limb motion, cold-induced vasoconstriction, etc., may cause inaccurate or failed heart rate/SpO₂ readings.
- Do not rely on readings in environments with strong infrared therapy that may interfere with optical measurement.

### 2. Non-Medical Statement

- Heart rate, SpO₂, sleep stages, temperature trends, ECG-related algorithm hints are for personal daily health reference only.
- **Not** for diagnosing, treating, preventing, or monitoring any disease, injury, or skin condition.
- Seek medical care immediately for palpitations, chest pain, dizziness, breathing difficulty—**do not rely on Device readings alone**.

### 3. ECG Feature

- ECG and arrhythmia risk hints are algorithm-assisted references **and cannot replace hospital ECG or physician diagnosis**.
- Follow in-app guidance and remain still during measurement.

---

## 5. Bluetooth and Wireless

- The Device uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate with your phone. In aircraft, hospitals, or other RF-restricted areas, follow local rules and disable related functions.
