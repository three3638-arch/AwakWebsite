## 1. Purpose and Background

This Authorization explains how we collect and use your physiological and behavioral health data when you use the Awak Health app and **Awak Health smart wearable devices** (the “Device”).

Under the Personal Information Protection Law of the PRC and Apple App Store requirements, **health data is sensitive personal information** requiring your **separate, explicit consent**. This Authorization supplements the Privacy Policy and does not replace the Privacy Policy or Terms of Service.

---

## 2. Authorized Data Types

After you tap “Agree and Continue,” we may collect the following via sensors, Bluetooth sync, and in-app entry (depending on your device model and enabled features):

| Category | Metrics |
|----------|---------|
| Heart & circulation | Heart rate, resting HR, HRV, SpO₂, ECG waveform summaries and related algorithm references |
| Activity & fitness | Steps, distance, calories, exercise type, session records, metabolic equivalent (MET) time series and activity load statistics (if device supports) |
| Sleep & recovery | Sleep/wake times, duration, light/deep/REM stages, quality-related scores |
| Body state | Skin temperature, stress index, blood pressure, glucose, uric acid, blood lipids (e.g. total cholesterol, triglycerides, HDL/LDL, if supported and enabled) |
| Body composition | Weight, BMI, body fat %, muscle mass/rate, body water, visceral fat level, basal metabolism, bone mass/protein and related measurements (if supported and enabled) |
| Comprehensive health check (micro checkup) | Multiple reference metrics combined in a single measurement flow (e.g. heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure, glucose, uric acid, lipids, stress/mood/fatigue, depending on device capability and items measured); this is a wearable health reference check, **not** a hospital physical exam or clinical laboratory test |
| Nutrition | Meal records, photo recognition data (only when you actively use it) |

**Virtual demo device**: If you use a “virtual demo device,” displayed data is **simulated**, not real measurements; all categories above may be shown as simulated data, tagged as demo. We may still process simulated data on servers in China for demo purposes.

---

## 3. Specific Uses of Data

Authorized data is used strictly for:

1. **Health display and reports**: Charts, daily/weekly/monthly trends, sleep and exercise analysis, body composition reports, micro checkup summaries, uric acid/lipid/MET trend charts via Awak proprietary algorithms and health models;
2. **Personalized health reference**: Exercise intensity, fatigue, nutrition, vitality, body composition, micro checkup, and metabolic load suggestions based on recovery, activity, sleep, and vitals (not medical prescriptions);
3. **Personalized suggestions (AI-assisted)**: After Awak core analysis, submit authorized health metrics, statistics, Awak summaries, body composition summaries, micro checkup summaries, uric acid/lipid trend summaries, MET/activity load summaries, and necessary basic body info (gender, date of birth or age range, height, weight) to **entrusted AI inference services in China**; **we do not** submit mobile number, name, ID, account ID, or ECG raw waveforms;
4. **Anomaly trend alerts**: Hints when metrics deviate from your baseline (**reference only, not medical diagnosis**);
5. **Awak model improvement (optional)**: Use **de-identified data that cannot re-identify you** to train and improve proprietary health algorithms. You may disable this in “Me → Privacy & Data Security → Health Data Authorization Management.”

---

## 4. Our Commitments

1. **No commercial misuse**: We do not sell or provide identifiable raw health data to advertisers or insurers;
2. **AI boundaries**: AI inference receives only health metrics, statistics, and summaries necessary for personalized suggestions, **not** directly identifiable information;
3. **Separate protection**: Health data is stored separately from account behavioral data, with encryption in transit and at rest;
4. **No cross-border transfer**: Health data is processed and stored in the People’s Republic of China;
5. **Transparency**: You may view authorization status in the App (see legal integration documentation).

---

## 5. Your Control

1. **Withdraw anytime**: Disable collection in “Me → Privacy & Data Security → Health Data Authorization Management” or turn off Bluetooth in system settings;
2. **Effects of withdrawal**: Stops new collection; prior lawful processing remains valid; you will no longer be able to use health monitoring and related core App features; to continue using the App, re-authorize or delete your account and exit;
3. **Deletion**: Request deletion of health-linked personal data via “Delete Account” (after 7-day cooling-off);
4. **Opt out of model improvement**: You may disable “de-identified analysis for improving Awak health models” in “Me → Privacy & Data Security → Health Data Authorization Management.”
